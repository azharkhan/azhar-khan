# azharkhan.net

A personal site: a homepage, a garden of things Azhar does, and the feeds that keep it current.

## Garden

**Pursuit**:
An ongoing interest (board games, films, cubing) that groups entries and has its own page.
_Avoid_: Category, hobby, section

**Entry**:
One thing in the garden with its own page, written by hand, fed from a source, or both.
_Avoid_: Post, item, card

**Wall**:
The garden's front page: a grid of entry cards, newest touch first.
_Avoid_: Feed, grid, index

**Thought**:
Short commentary attached to an entry, such as a play comment or a review.
_Avoid_: Note (a note is an entry kind), comment

## Board games

**Game**:
A board game entry, keyed by its BoardGameGeek id.
_Avoid_: Title, thing

**Play**:
One logged session of a game on a date, possibly counting several plays.
_Avoid_: Session, log

**Played game**:
A game with at least one play; appears on the timeline and may appear on the wall.
_Avoid_: Active game

**Shelf-only game**:
An owned game with no plays; has a page but stays off the timeline, and off the wall unless reviewed or written up.
_Avoid_: Unplayed, backlog, pile of shame

**Review**:
Azhar's public written verdict on a game, kept on BoardGameGeek.
_Avoid_: Comment, rating, thought

**Shelf**:
The set of games Azhar currently owns.
_Avoid_: Collection, library

**Game details**:
Reference facts about a game (year, players, time, weight, designer, rank, box art), kept in a committed snapshot.
_Avoid_: Metadata, stats
